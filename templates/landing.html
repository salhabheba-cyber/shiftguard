How it works

Create your account

Tell us your business name and choose a sign-in email and password. We approve new businesses quickly.

Add your team

Add employees with their shifts, working days, salary and a 4-digit PIN. Add branches if you have more than one.

Open the kiosk

Put the kiosk on any tablet at the entrance. Attendance, payroll and reports fill in on their own.
